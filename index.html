GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are MoQR codes free forever?

Yes. MoQR creates standard, static QR codes. You can download them freely without an account.

Will a QR code expire?

No. The QR image does not expire, but a linked website or other destination may stop working.

Can I add any logo and colors?

You can customize both. Very large logos or low-contrast colors may prevent scanning, so test with more than one camera before printing.
